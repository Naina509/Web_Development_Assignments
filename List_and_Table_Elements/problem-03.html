Academic Rules

Mandatory: Students must maintain the required attendance.

Important Academic Notice: Internal assessments must be completed before the deadline.

Classroom Rules

  • Maintain discipline in the classroom.
  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Keep mobile phones on silent mode.
  • Submit assignments on time.
  • Respect faculty and classmates.

Sequence Before Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Understand the experiment.
  3. Prepare the required program or materials.
  4. Attend the practical class.
  5. Submit the practical record.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A theory-based teaching session.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments.
Tutorial
A guided session for solving problems and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the academic semester.
Practical
A hands-on session for applying theoretical concepts.
