Welcome to National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a major technology and career event where students, graduates and professionals can explore career opportunities, internships, workshops and the latest developments in technology.

Meet leading organizations, attend expert sessions, participate in workshops and discover your next career opportunity.

Message from the Organizer

Our goal is to connect talented students with industry leaders and provide practical knowledge about technology and careers.

We welcome students from engineering, management, computer science, design and other disciplines.

"Your career begins with curiosity, learning and the courage to take the first step."

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating companies.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Carry your student or professional identification.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Attend workshops according to your interests.
  • Dress professionally for company interactions.
  • Follow up with recruiters after the expo.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the expo.
  2. Select workshops and sessions.
  3. Explore participating organizations.
  4. Attend career talks.
  5. Meet recruiters and speakers.
  6. Apply for internships or job opportunities.
  7. Network with other participants.
  8. Complete the expo feedback.
