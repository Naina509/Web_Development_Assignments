Registration & Training Steps

  1. Complete the athlete registration form.
  2. Select your preferred sport.
  3. Attend the skill assessment.
  4. Choose a suitable training program.
  5. Start regular training sessions.
  6. Participate in academy tournaments.

Sports Categories

Match
A competitive game between athletes or teams.
Fixture
A scheduled match between participating teams.
League
A competition where teams play multiple matches.
Knockout
A tournament format where the losing team is eliminated.
