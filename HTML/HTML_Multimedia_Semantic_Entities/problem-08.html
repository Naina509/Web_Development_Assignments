Understanding Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account helps you safely store money and earn interest on your balance.

  • Suitable for regular savings
  • Easy access to money
  • Interest can be earned
  • Online banking facilities

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions and business activities.

Understanding Credit Cards

A credit card allows you to make purchases using a credit limit provided by the bank.

  • Use credit responsibly
  • Pay bills on time
  • Avoid unnecessary debt
  • Understand interest and late charges
"Spend wisely today to build a stronger financial future."

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan provides funds that can be repaid through scheduled instalments.

Home Loan

A home loan can help eligible customers finance the purchase or construction of a home.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save a portion of your income regularly.
  • Build an emergency fund.
  • Pay bills and loan instalments on time.
  • Compare charges before selecting a product.
  • Avoid unnecessary borrowing.
  • Protect your banking passwords and PIN.
  • Review your expenses regularly.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Select a suitable account.
  2. Check eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Complete verification.
  6. Receive your account details.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on borrowed money or paid on eligible deposits.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or financial product is maintained.

Responsible Money Management

Budgeting helps you understand where your money goes.

Always save before spending whenever possible.

A simple financial goal can make a big difference over time.

"Small savings today can become meaningful financial security tomorrow."

Simple EMI Example

Suppose the principal amount is ₹1,00,000.

If the annual interest rate is 10%, then:

Monthly Rate = 10 ÷ 12

Total Amount = Principal + Interest

Example: 100 × 10 = 1000

Therefore, understanding the rate and tenure is important before taking a loan.
