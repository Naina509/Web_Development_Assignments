Destination Overview

Welcome to Jaipur, the beautiful Pink City of Rajasthan. Jaipur is famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colorful markets and rich cultural heritage.

Explore historic landmarks, enjoy traditional Rajasthani food and experience the warm hospitality of the city.

Top Attractions

Jaipur offers many exciting places to visit and memorable experiences.

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Nahargarh Fort
  • Jal Mahal

One-Day Jaipur Itinerary

  1. Visit Amber Fort in the morning.
  2. Explore Jal Mahal.
  3. Visit City Palace.
  4. Explore Jantar Mantar.
  5. See Hawa Mahal.
  6. Enjoy shopping at local markets.
  7. Have a traditional Rajasthani dinner.

Travel Tips

  • Carry a water bottle and stay hydrated.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Carry sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • Keep your valuables safe.
  • Respect local customs and heritage sites.
