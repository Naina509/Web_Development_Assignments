Welcome to Spice Garden

Spice Garden is a family restaurant serving fresh, delicious and hygienic food. We offer dine-in, takeaway and home delivery.

Our food is prepared with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes to give you an unforgettable experience.

Our Restaurant Story

Spice Garden started in 2018 with a simple goal of serving quality food at affordable prices.

Today, we serve hundreds of customers every day with Indian, Chinese and continental dishes.

"Good food brings people together and creates beautiful memories."

Our Chef

Chef Arjun has more than 12 years of culinary experience and specializes in Indian cuisine.
