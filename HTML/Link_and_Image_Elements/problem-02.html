College Launches New Innovation Hub for Student Entrepreneurs

New campus center aims to turn student ideas into real-world solutions

Students at Greenfield College have officially opened a new Innovation Hub, designed to support entrepreneurship, technology projects, and creative student-led initiatives. The new facility opened on September 2, 2026 at the college's main campus in Boston, Massachusetts.

The Innovation Hub includes collaborative workspaces, computer facilities, meeting rooms, and areas where students can develop prototypes and business ideas. College officials said the facility will provide students with access to resources that were previously difficult to find in one location. The goal is to encourage students to transform classroom knowledge into practical projects.

The center was created in response to growing interest in entrepreneurship and technology among students. According to the college, more than 500 students are expected to use the facility during its first academic year. Faculty members and local professionals will also participate in workshops covering topics such as business planning, software development, marketing, and leadership.

College administrators believe the hub could strengthen connections between students and the local business community. The initiative is intended to give students practical experience while helping promising ideas develop into projects, startups, or community solutions. The college plans to organize regular competitions and demonstration events where students can present their work.
