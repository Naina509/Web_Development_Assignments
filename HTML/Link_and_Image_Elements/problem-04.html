Welcome to Agra

Agra is one of India's most famous heritage destinations and is located on the banks of the Yamuna River. It is best known for the magnificent Taj Mahal, but the city also offers impressive forts, Mughal architecture, traditional markets and delicious local food.

A weekend is enough to experience many of Agra's highlights while enjoying its rich history and culture.

1. Top Attractions

Agra has several important historical attractions that showcase the architectural achievements of the Mughal period.

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a world-famous marble monument built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Sunrise and sunset are particularly beautiful times to see the monument.

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is a massive red sandstone fort containing palaces, halls and courtyards associated with several Mughal rulers.

Learn more about the Agra Fort on UNESCO's official website .

Fatehpur Sikri

Located outside Agra, Fatehpur Sikri is a historic Mughal city known for its impressive sandstone buildings and the enormous Buland Darwaza.

Read about Fatehpur Sikri on UNESCO .

2. Culture and Local Experiences

Agra's culture reflects centuries of Mughal, Persian and North Indian influences. Visitors can explore traditional bazaars, handicraft shops and historic neighborhoods.

Marble inlay work, often known as pietra dura, is one of the city's famous traditional crafts. Visitors can see artisans creating decorative objects using colored stones and marble.

Popular markets include Sadar Bazaar and Kinari Bazaar, where travelers can look for handicrafts, souvenirs, leather goods and traditional clothing.

3. Food to Try

A trip to Agra is incomplete without trying some of its famous local dishes and sweets.

  • Petha – a famous translucent sweet associated with Agra.
  • Bedai and Jalebi – a popular North Indian breakfast combination.
  • Mughlai Cuisine – rich curries, kebabs and aromatic rice dishes.
  • Tandoori dishes – grilled foods cooked in a traditional clay oven.

For the best experience, try local food from well-reviewed restaurants and established food markets.

4. Best Time to Visit

The most comfortable period for exploring Agra is generally from October to March, when temperatures are more pleasant for sightseeing.

Winter mornings can be cool, so carry a light jacket. Summer months can become extremely hot, making outdoor sightseeing less comfortable.

If possible, visit the Taj Mahal early in the morning to avoid the strongest afternoon heat and enjoy softer light for photography.

5. Travel Tips

Important travel tip: Start your sightseeing early, especially when visiting the Taj Mahal, and carry drinking water, comfortable footwear and sun protection.

  • Wear comfortable shoes because several attractions require walking.
  • Carry a valid photo ID when visiting major attractions.
  • Keep your belongings secure in crowded markets.
  • Check monument opening days and ticket rules before traveling.
  • Use registered taxis, reputable ride services or trusted local guides.
