Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of talent, creativity, technology and teamwork. Students from different colleges are invited to participate in exciting competitions and events.

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest brings students together for three days of competitions, cultural activities, technical events and entertainment. Participants can register for individual or team events and compete for exciting prizes.

Venue: ABC College Campus | Dates: 15–17 January 2026

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official college fest registration page.
  2. Choose the competition you want to participate in.
  3. Enter your name, college and contact details.
  4. Select individual or team participation.
  5. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save the registration/reference number for future use.

Registration Deadline: 10 January 2026

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID.
  • Participants must report at least 30 minutes before the event.
  • Teams must follow the maximum team size specified for each event.
  • Use of unfair means will result in disqualification.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • The decision of the judges will be final.

Event Definitions

Open Event
An event that is open to all eligible college students.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
An event in which a single participant competes.
Registration
The process of officially signing up for a competition.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing and managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where finalists compete for the winning position.
