How to Book an Appointment

  1. Choose your preferred department.
  2. Select a doctor and suitable date.
  3. Complete the appointment request form.
  4. Provide your contact information.
  5. Wait for appointment confirmation.

Appointment requests are subject to doctor availability.

Patient Instructions

Before Your Visit

  • Carry a valid identification document.
  • Bring previous medical reports if relevant.
  • Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment.
  • Keep your contact information updated.
  • Inform the clinic if you need to reschedule.

After Your Consultation

Follow the instructions provided by your healthcare professional and attend follow-up appointments when advised.

This website provides general clinic information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
