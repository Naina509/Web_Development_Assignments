Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a major career and education event designed for students, graduates and job seekers. Participants can meet recruiters, explore academic programs, discover internships and learn about different career paths.

The fair provides an opportunity to connect with universities, companies, government organizations, training institutes and industry professionals.

Registration is open to students and job seekers.

Event Objectives

  • Connect students with employers and educational institutions.
  • Provide information about career opportunities.
  • Help job seekers discover internships and placements.
  • Introduce scholarship and apprenticeship opportunities.
  • Provide career preparation and guidance.
  • Encourage networking with industry professionals.

Organizer's Message

"Our goal is to create a platform where every participant can discover meaningful opportunities, develop confidence and take the next step toward a successful career."

The fair welcomes participants from schools, colleges, universities and professional backgrounds.

Participating Organizations

Leading organizations from different sectors will participate in the fair and provide information about their opportunities.

Technology Organizations

Technology companies will showcase opportunities in software development, data science, cloud computing, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.

Education Institutions

Universities and training institutions will provide information about degree programs, certifications and scholarships.

Government & Public Sector

Public-sector organizations will provide information about government careers, examinations and apprenticeship programs.

Preparation Guide

Preparing before attending the fair can help you make the most of your time and interact confidently with organizations.

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research organizations you are interested in.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Carry copies of your resume.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and educators.
  • Keep your educational certificates available if required.
  • Plan which sessions you want to attend.

Attendee Tip

Arrive early, check the event map and schedule, and prioritize organizations that match your career interests.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the fair.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue and complete check-in.
  4. Attend career and education sessions.
  5. Meet participating organizations.
  6. Submit applications or resumes where appropriate.
  7. Network with professionals and other participants.
  8. Follow up with organizations after the event.

Information & FAQs

Career Fair Terminology

Career Fair
An event where employers and job seekers meet to discuss career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work opportunity that provides practical experience.
Placement
Assistance provided to candidates in finding employment opportunities.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for education.
Apprenticeship
A structured program combining practical workplace experience with learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the career fair?

Students, graduates, job seekers and individuals exploring education or career opportunities can attend.


Is registration required?

Yes. Participants should complete the online registration form before attending.


Should I bring a resume?

Yes. Job seekers are encouraged to bring an updated resume when meeting employers.


Can students attend education sessions?

Yes. Students can attend sessions related to higher education, scholarships, internships and career planning.


Can I attend multiple sessions?

Yes. Participants may choose sessions according to the published event schedule.
